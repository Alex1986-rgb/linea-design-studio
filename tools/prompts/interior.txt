TASK:
Photorealistic interior photograph of one room, taken at eye level, about 1.6 m above the floor, with a 24 mm wide-angle lens held perfectly level so vertical lines stay vertical.

COMPOSITION:
The camera stands at the room entrance looking into the room, so the viewer reads the whole space: floor, two walls and the main furniture group. Foreground, middle ground and background are all present, giving the image depth.
